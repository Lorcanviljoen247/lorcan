About me

Hi, my name is Lorcan, and I’ve chosen the hawk as my spirit animal because it symbolises strength, grace, and a connection to the divine—values that are important to me. This year marks my final year of high school, and I’m excited to finish school and pursue a design course, hopefully at UJ. I’m eager to learn what it takes to become a designer, inspired by professionals like Jako and his team. I enjoy creating things from scratch and collaborating with others, as it allows me to express my creativity and ingenuity through the planning and designing of functional projects.

The frist thing I learned :

The first thing I learned was how interconnected everyone’s work is in this field. Designers are ultimately responsible for the overall structure and aesthetics of a website or app, while also understanding the limitations of what is possible and what isn’t for developers to create. This collaboration is crucial for ensuring a successful end product

The second thing I learned :

As a person wanting to start a career in design a great portfolio is curial for your resume if you want get a job as a designer

The third thing I learned :

A holistic approach to this career is essential, as it’s important to understand the entire process of bringing a finished product to life. From sitting with the client and brainstorming the website design, to wireframing as a designer, and acknowledging the hard work of developers and even the QA team, each step plays a crucial role. Gaining a clear understanding of what happens at every stage will go a long way in setting me up for success in this field in the future.
